# Assessment Guide — AI Baseline

## Overview

The AI Baseline assessment is a built-in Compliance Manager template addressing responsible AI governance controls relevant to organizations deploying AI-powered Microsoft 365 features (for example, Microsoft Copilot and related generative AI capabilities). It reflects Microsoft's growing investment in AI-specific regulatory templates, alongside premium options such as the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 23894:2023, ISO/IEC 42001:2023, and the EU Artificial Intelligence Act.

## Why This Assessment Matters

AI governance is one of the fastest-evolving areas of the Compliance Manager regulation catalog. The prominent in-portal banner — *"AI regulation templates can help you create assessments that comply with the latest standards and best practices in the field of AI"* — reflects Microsoft's active expansion of this space. Organizations rolling out AI-powered Microsoft 365 capabilities should treat this assessment as a governance prerequisite, not an optional add-on, particularly where AI features process regulated or sensitive data.

## Observed Improvement Action Categories

Improvement actions associated with the AI Baseline assessment in this tenant span:

- Implement risk scoring for AI prompts and interactions
- Apply retention labels to AI-generated content
- Manage user consent to organizational data access by AI features
- Enhance case review processes using AI-assisted discovery
- Accelerate alert triage with AI-generated insights

These actions span **Manage device compliance, Protect against threats, Control access, Govern information,** and **Manage insider risk** categories, reflecting the cross-cutting nature of AI governance — it is not a single control domain but touches identity, data governance, and information protection simultaneously.

## Working This Assessment

1. Note that **all 96 Microsoft-managed actions are already complete** — Microsoft has fully satisfied its shared-responsibility obligations for this template already
2. Focus entirely on the **80 customer-managed actions**, of which only 2 are complete — this is the highest-leverage remaining work in the entire tenant's improvement action backlog on a percentage basis
3. Prioritize actions tied to **data governance and retention** first, since AI features frequently surface or generate content that must be classified and retained correctly
4. Coordinate with the Data Governance and Information Protection teams, since AI Baseline actions frequently overlap with Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management and Information Protection capabilities already in use elsewhere in the tenant

## Recommended Next Steps

- Evaluate whether premium AI regulation templates (ISO/IEC 42001:2023, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act) are relevant given the organization's AI deployment scope and geography
- Establish an AI governance owner distinct from general data protection ownership, given the specialized nature of these controls
- Revisit this assessment quarterly, given the pace at which Microsoft updates AI-related regulatory templates
